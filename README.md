# PROOFLYN
### An MCP-Powered Invoice Dispute Investigation Agent

**PROOFLYN** is an AI-powered invoice dispute investigation agent designed to help B2B suppliers and MSMEs investigate delayed or disputed payments through deterministic reconciliation, source-linked evidence, and evidence gap detection.

Instead of manually searching through purchase orders, invoices, delivery receipts, payment records, and business communications, PROOFLYN brings relevant evidence together to help users understand and investigate invoice discrepancies.

---

## Problem Statement

Small and medium-sized businesses frequently face delayed payments and invoice disputes. Relevant records are often scattered across multiple documents, spreadsheets, emails, and communication channels.

Manually reconstructing what happened takes time, creates operational friction, and makes it difficult to respond to buyer disputes with reliable supporting evidence.

## Our Solution

PROOFLYN provides a controlled investigation workflow that connects financial records, identifies discrepancies, and generates verifiable, evidence-backed findings.

Our goal is to make invoice dispute investigation faster, more transparent, and more reliable.

## Key Features

- **MCP-Powered Investigation:** Enables an AI agent to investigate invoice-related information through structured tools.
- **Deterministic Reconciliation:** Compares purchase orders, invoices, and delivery records using rule-based validation.
- **Evidence Graph:** Connects relevant documents and transaction events to provide a clear view of supporting evidence.
- **Evidence Gap Detection:** Identifies missing, incomplete, or inconsistent records without assuming that missing evidence never existed.
- **Source-Linked Findings:** Keeps investigation results traceable to their original supporting documents.
- **Human-in-the-Loop Control:** Keeps important decisions and external actions under human review.

## How It Works

1. **Provide Documents:** Upload relevant purchase orders, invoices, delivery receipts, and payment records.
2. **Extract Information:** Identify important transaction details from the provided records.
3. **Investigate:** Use MCP-powered tools to retrieve and examine relevant information.
4. **Reconcile:** Apply deterministic rules to identify mismatches and discrepancies.
5. **Detect Evidence Gaps:** Identify missing or inconsistent supporting records.
6. **Generate Findings:** Produce a structured investigation summary with source-linked evidence.

## Technology Stack

The intended architecture focuses on:

- TypeScript
- AI / Large Language Models
- Model Context Protocol (MCP)
- Deterministic Reconciliation Engine
- Document Intelligence
- Evidence Graph

*Specific frameworks, databases, and service integrations will be documented as the implementation evolves.*

## Core Principles

- Accuracy over assumptions.
- Deterministic financial calculations.
- Transparent and traceable evidence.
- No fabricated investigation findings.
- Human approval for consequential actions.
- Clear distinction between missing evidence and confirmed discrepancies.

## Project Vision

Our vision is to build a trustworthy investigation layer for B2B financial disputes, helping suppliers and MSMEs reconstruct transaction histories, identify discrepancies, and prepare evidence-backed responses.

**PROOFLYN — Don't just chase payments. Prove what happened.**
